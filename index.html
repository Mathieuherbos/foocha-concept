Tales from Foocha

Two kids of immigrants who missed the food from home.

Foocha's founders grew up between two cultures — and two kitchens. They opened a canteen in the center of Brussels to share the dishes they actually grew up eating, the kind you won't find on most "Asian fusion" menus in town.

No shortcuts, nothing frozen — just the cooking you'd find in your grandmother's kitchen, if your grandmother had impeccable plating and a soft spot for yuzu lemonade.

— the Foocha family

Before you ask

Fooch, actually.

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes — but tables for groups of four or more go fast on weekends. Booking online is the safer move if you're planning ahead.

Is there a vegetarian option?

Yes. A few dishes on the menu are vegetarian, and others can be adjusted on request — just mention it when you order.

Do you deliver?

Not yet. Foocha is dine-in and takeaway only, straight from the canteen.

Can I buy the apparel without eating here?

Yes — the apparel shop ships on its own, no reservation needed.
