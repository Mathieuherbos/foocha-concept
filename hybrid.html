Tales from Foocha

Two kids who missed home.

Foocha's founders grew up between two cultures — and two kitchens. They opened a canteen on Rue de Flandre to share the dishes they actually grew up eating.

No shortcuts, nothing frozen — just the cooking you'd find in your grandmother's kitchen, if your grandmother had impeccable plating and a soft spot for yuzu lemonade.

— the Foocha family
